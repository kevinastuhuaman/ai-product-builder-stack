# Stack marks

Tool cards and their evidence dialogs share the same mark. Simple Icons supplies the configured vendor paths. Locally vendored logos fill the gaps left by missing or removed Simple Icons entries, including OpenAI, Azure, AWS, Langfuse and Playwright. Azure and AWS service entries use the parent brand, rather than inventing a service logo.

Asset origins are recorded in `public/assets/brands/sources.json`. Lobe Icons and SVGL assets are MIT licensed, gilbarbara/logos assets are CC0, Gatus is Apache-2.0 and Healthchecks is BSD-3-Clause. The corresponding notices are retained under `public/licenses/`. Existing Kevin-owned portfolio brand assets are copied from the recorded public commit. Brand marks identify tools and do not imply endorsement.

Custom agent harness and human approval gates are concepts, so they use Lucide line icons. Fireflies, Parallel and Clay also use deliberate line icons until a redistributable brand asset is available. Their evidence and lifecycle records are unchanged.
